## general
Eat plenty of vegetables, fruit, whole grains (millets, brown rice, whole wheat roti), dal and pulses.
Keep salt under one teaspoon (5 g) a day in total. Aim for 30 minutes of gentle activity, such as walking, on most days.
Sleep 7 to 8 hours, avoid tobacco, and limit alcohol.

## high_cholesterol
Choose oats, barley, rajma, chana, flaxseed, almonds and walnuts. Cook with small amounts of mustard, groundnut or rice bran oil.
Limit ghee, butter, vanaspati, fried snacks (samosa, pakora), red meat, bakery items and full-fat sweets.

## high_bp
Follow a low-salt, DASH-style diet: avoid pickles, papad, namkeen, packaged soups and instant noodles.
Eat potassium-rich foods such as banana, coconut water, spinach and tomato, unless a doctor has said to limit potassium because of kidney disease.
Practise slow breathing or pranayama for 10 minutes a day.

## high_sugar
Prefer whole grains and millets over white rice and maida. Avoid sugary drinks, sweets and fruit juice; eat whole fruit instead.
Include protein (dal, paneer, eggs, curd) at every meal. A 10-minute walk after meals helps lower sugar.

## low_hdl
Regular brisk walking raises HDL. Include nuts, seeds and fish if non-vegetarian. Avoid trans fats such as vanaspati.

## high_triglycerides
Cut sugar, sweets, white rice and alcohol. Eat smaller portions of carbohydrates and more vegetables.

## heart_rhythm
Avoid too much tea, coffee and energy drinks. Report palpitations, dizziness or fainting to a doctor promptly.

## exercise_caution
If there is chest pain on exertion or an abnormal treadmill test, do only light activity such as slow walking until a cardiologist approves more.
